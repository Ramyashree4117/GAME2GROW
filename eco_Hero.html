How to Play

🌍 The Objective

Your goal is to become an Eco Hero by cleaning up all the locations on the World Map. Each location has several missions for you to complete.

👆 Gameplay

To complete a mission, drag items from the play area to the correct action bins at the bottom of the screen.

✅ Actions

  • Sort: Drag trash (🗑️), recyclables (♻️), and compost (🌿) to the right bins.
  • Plant & Grow: Some levels require you to plant saplings (🌱) or water plants (💧).

🏆 Winning

Complete all the missions in a location to clean it up and earn your final score for that area!
