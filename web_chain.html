How to Play

1. Your goal is to build a food chain from producer to top predator.

2. Drag items from the bottom pool into the empty slots at the top.

3. The chain starts with what gets eaten first (usually a plant).

4. If you get stuck, click the button once for a hint, and a second time for the full answer and an explanation.

5. Once you're ready, hit the "Check Chain" button!
